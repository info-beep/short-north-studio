## Grammar

**Typographic poster.** *Authored.* The brand's real asset with no photography
on hand is its voice plus one colour: skin. The ground is most of the frame, so the
ground can be the protagonist. Why the other seven lost:

- Filmic one-shot: needs footage for `scrub`; we have none, and it is the default drift.
- Chaptered editorial: wants long-form substance; this is a 40-second decision page.
- Live surface: there is no software product.
- Continuous world: needs one generated or filmed geography.
- Gallery/catalog: four levels with no photos or prices is a thin collection.
- Split stage: before/after was close, but a 50/50 split needs real before/after photos to be honest.
- Rhythmic cutlist: the peak needs a held, slow deepening; cuts would kill it.

## Signature move: the tan line

A soft-edged vertical strap of the original pale skin colour follows the pointer
across the page (desktop), sitting under the type like skin under a swimsuit strap.
Early on it is invisible (pale on pale). By VIP it is a stark stripe: the visible
difference between where you started and where you are. On touch, tapping the shade
rail's "Tan line" control shows the strap for a few seconds. Coded in the page,
driven by the page's own scroll-to-shade map. The engine is untouched.

dead scroll; the verification pass should not count it as a defect.
