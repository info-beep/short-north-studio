Privacy Policy

Draft updated October 10, 2026

Draft for review. This policy describes the website features identified during preparation. Our data practices and this wording need review by the business and a qualified privacy professional.

This page explains how information is handled when you visit short-north-studio.com or ask about an event at Short North Studio, 612 N High St, Columbus, OH 43215.

Event inquiries and estimates

Our estimate form asks for your name, email address, phone number and event details. These may include the event type, package, date, start time, guest count, duration, add-ons, estimated price, deposit, honoree and any notes you provide. Please avoid including sensitive personal information or unnecessary details about children.

We use inquiry information to respond, discuss availability and pricing, and help arrange your event. Submitting an estimate request does not confirm a reservation. The form is configured to submit through Netlify Forms. If you use a phone or text link instead, your telephone or messaging provider handles that communication.

Website hosting and technical information

Netlify hosts this website and processes form submissions. Hosting and other website providers may process technical information, such as IP address, browser and device information, requested pages and access times, to deliver and operate their services. See Netlify’s privacy policy.

Google Tag Manager

The website uses Google Tag Manager to load and manage website tags. The information collected depends on which tags are enabled; tags may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement or marketing. The current container configuration still needs review to identify all active tags and any consent requirements. See Google’s Tag Manager privacy information.

Metricool

Some pages include a Metricool tracking image to help measure website visits. Loading it sends a request to Metricool, which can receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser information. See Metricool’s privacy policy.

Google Fonts

Some pages load fonts from Google. Your browser contacts Google’s servers to retrieve those fonts and sends technical request information, including your IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Peerspace bookings

Booking links take you to Peerspace. Information you enter there, including account, booking and payment information, is handled under Peerspace’s privacy policy. Peerspace may share booking information with us so we can host your event. This estimate form does not ask for payment-card details.

Your choices and requests

You can adjust cookies and tracking controls in your browser. Blocking third-party requests may affect website features, and blocking cookies alone does not stop every tracking request. You can call us instead of using the estimate form. For questions or requests about information you have provided, call 614-414-2205 or write to the studio at the address above. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Retention, sharing and policy updates

Service providers involved in hosting, inquiries and bookings process information as described above. Specific retention periods, other sharing practices and request-handling procedures still need business confirmation before this draft is finalized. Updates will be reflected in the date on this page.
