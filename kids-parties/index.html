Parents ask

the fine print.

How long is a party?

Two hours in a private studio. The studio is yours for the whole time.

What ages is it for?

Our parties are designed for kids ages 5 to 11.

How many kids can come?

The Squishy Party includes up to 8 kids. The Neon and Princess parties include up to 10. You can add more for $25 each, up to 15 kids total.

Do parents stay?

Yes. A parent or guardian is required at the party.

Can we bring cake and snacks?

Yes. There's room for cake, snacks, and gifts.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. A $50 non-refundable deposit reserves your date.

What if I pay and my date is taken?

We text you after every booking to confirm your date and time. If your date is already booked, we'll move your party to another date that works for you.

Do I have to set up or clean up?

No. Supplies are set before you arrive, and we handle all the cleanup after.

Where is the studio?

612 N. High St. in the Short North Arts District, Columbus, OH 43215.

How do I book?

Call or text 614-414-2205 with your preferred date, theme, and head count. We'll confirm availability and get you set.
